Field service · House case

More revenue per hour.
Less return.

Emergency jobs invoiced about CAD 286 per onsite hour, compared with CAD 181 for scheduled work. After recorded parts, travel and callbacks, the result reversed: about CAD 80 versus CAD 119 per all-in recorded hour.

The recommendation: a 13-week reversible test capped at CAD 5,000 before making a CAD 85,000 commitment.

Industrial laundry · House caseThe bottleneck was upstream of the dryers.+

Athena classified idle entries in the machine log by reason: 48 were maintenance, 866 were waiting for upstream load release and 475 were scheduling gaps. The records pointed upstream of drying.

Recommendation: a 90-day verification and a shadow schedule before committing to another dryer.

Hospital · House caseThe staffing total hid the busiest hour.+

Daily staffing showed five nurses, but lunch coverage cut capacity when demand peaked. Protecting coverage reduced the modelled wait from about 23 minutes to 6.

Recommendation: redesign coverage with existing staff. The limit was explicit: the analysis covered one observed day.
